Research Interests

  • 3D Reconstruction & Generation Recovering and synthesizing high-fidelity 3D geometry, PBR materials, and structured parts from images, video, or point clouds.
  • Implicit Neural Representations Encoding scene properties such as SDF geometry, radiance, wavelet primitives, and motion as continuous neural fields.
  • Computer Vision Enabling machines to interpret visual signals, spanning multi-view geometry, neural rendering, and physical signal blending.
  • Artificial Intelligence Diffusion transformers, flow matching, and deep optimization methods for scalable visual and spatial intelligence.
